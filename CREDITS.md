## 📚 Academic & Algorithmic Foundations

- **Leon A. Gatys, Alexander S. Ecker, and Matthias Bethge (2015).**  
  *A Neural Algorithm of Artistic Style.* [arXiv:1508.06576](https://arxiv.org/abs/1508.06576).
- **Leon A. Gatys, Alexander S. Ecker, and Matthias Bethge (2016).**  
  *Image Style Transfer Using Convolutional Neural Networks.* IEEE Conference on Computer Vision and Pattern Recognition (CVPR 2016).
- **TensorFlow Neural Style Transfer Tutorial:**  
  Originally authored by the TensorFlow team under Apache License 2.0. Neural Canvas ports the pipeline to pure PyTorch with modern architectural calibration.
  Source: [TensorFlow Neural Style Transfer Tutorial](https://www.tensorflow.org/tutorials/generative/style_transfer)
- **Torchvision Pretrained VGG19 (ImageNet-1K V1 Weights):**  
  Pretrained convolutional feature representations provided by torchvision under the BSD-3-Clause License.  
  Documentation & Weights: [torchvision.models.vgg19](https://pytorch.org/vision/stable/models/generated/torchvision.models.vgg19.html) (Weights: `VGG19_Weights.IMAGENET1K_V1`).
